# Guide to TE analysis software, by analysis step

This page reorganises the 198 software tools, databases and workflows in the catalogue by analysis step. It starts with the complete pipelines and the steps each one covers, then lists the tools for each step, and ends with visualization. Every entry gives the original paper, the lab named on that paper, and one objective authority indicator. The default route is unchanged: EDTA, then TEtrimmer, then TEsorter, then a CDS-gated trusted library, then soft-masking or panEDTA (see [`docs/WORKFLOW.md`](../WORKFLOW.md)). The Chinese version is [README.zh.md](README.zh.md).

## How to read this page

- **Role**: only tools that this repository already names as its default are marked "repository default". Everything else is "alternative or complement". Where a step has no default, rows are ordered by citation count, which does not say which tool suits your genome.
- **Original paper**: title, journal and year come from OpenAlex. The DOI was checked against the Crossref title for 171 entries; the rest resolve only in OpenAlex and are marked in the `doi_check` column of `catalog/SOFTWARE_AUTHORITY.tsv`. Entries without a title-matched paper show the official link only.
- **Lab**: the people flagged as corresponding authors in the paper metadata, with their institutions; the last author when no corresponding author is flagged; the first author is listed as well. These are names on the paper, not a judgement of the lab.
- **Authority indicator**: the OpenAlex citation count of the paper (retrieved 2026-11-25), plus a tier from the fixed rule below. There is no subjective praise.

### Tier rule

| Tier | Rule |
|---|---|
| standard | 500 or more citations |
| established | 100 to 499 citations |
| newer | fewer than 100 citations and the paper is from 2023 or later |
| niche | fewer than 100 citations and the paper is from before 2023 |
| no paper | the catalogue has no title-matched paper |

Citation counts do not depend on the field of the paper. A general-purpose tool such as BEDTools, MMseqs2 or IGV has counts from every discipline, so its tier does not describe its standing in TE analysis. Counts change over time; the per-entry data are in [`catalog/SOFTWARE_AUTHORITY.tsv`](../../catalog/SOFTWARE_AUTHORITY.tsv).

This page has 198 entries: standard 28, established 47, newer 42, niche 58, no paper 23.
