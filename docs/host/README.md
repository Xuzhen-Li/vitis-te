## 5. TEs and the host

<!-- PROSE:SECTION:T00D -->
**Effects on genes.** A new insertion can disrupt a coding sequence, alter splicing, or land near a gene and change when or where that gene is expressed. TEs can also donate regulatory sequence. Feschotte et al. (2002) and Lisch (2013) review these outcomes in plants. Not every insertion matters; many land in already repetitive DNA and remain silent.

**Silencing.** Eukaryotic hosts use overlapping epigenetic mechanisms to silence TE expression and mobility (Slotkin and Martienssen 2007, [doi:10.1038/nrg2072](https://doi.org/10.1038/nrg2072), abstract: "Overlapping epigenetic mechanisms have evolved in eukaryotic cells to silence the expression and mobility of transposable elements"). In plants, RNA-directed DNA methylation (RdDM) is the major small RNA-mediated epigenetic pathway (Matzke and Mosher 2014, [doi:10.1038/nrg3683](https://doi.org/10.1038/nrg3683), abstract). Silencing protects the genome, and because TEs recruit the silencing machinery, they can also shape epigenetic states at single genes and across larger chromosomal regions (Slotkin and Martienssen 2007).

**A grapevine trait example: berry skin colour.** Kobayashi, Goto-Yamamoto and Hirochika (2004, [doi:10.1126/science.1095011](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1095011)) reported retrotransposon-induced mutations in grape skin color; the body of that short paper was not re-read on 2026-11-15, so gene and element names are not taken from it. This et al. (2007, [doi:10.1007/s00122-006-0472-2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00122-006-0472-2)) found, across over 200 *V. vinifera* accessions, that insertion of the retroelement Gret1 in the promoter of *VvmybA1*, a regulator of anthocyanin biosynthesis, was strongly associated with the white-fruited phenotype. That association alone does not establish that the insertion caused the colour change. The same abstract reports that some pigmented alleles are consistent with later “excision” of Gret1 (the authors' word). Because Gret1 is an LTR retrotransposon, it moves by copy-and-paste rather than transposase-mediated excision; loss of such an element usually occurs through recombination between the two LTRs, leaving a solo LTR. Whether the coloured alleles in that study actually carry a solo LTR is [待确认]. The abstract also notes that the association of Gret1 with the white-fruited phenotype was rarely absent, suggesting that a white phenotype can also arise through mutation in additional genes. *VvmybA1* is discussed there in the context of a trait under human selection after domestication, rather than as proof of the insertion's role in the original domestication event.

**Somatic variation in a clonal crop.** Because grapevine is usually propagated by cuttings, a somatic change in one bud can be maintained as a new clone. Azuma et al. (2009, [doi:10.1016/j.plantsci.2008.12.015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.plantsci.2008.12.015)) studied bud sports of white-skinned 'Italia'. For colour recovery in 'Ruby Okuyama', they wrote that it "may have occurred as a result of intra-LTR (long terminal repeat) recombination within a retrotransposon, Gret1". In plain words, the two long terminal repeats of a single element can recombine with each other, deleting the internal sequence and leaving behind a single LTR. That sentence in Azuma 2009 restates earlier work; a candidate source is Kobayashi et al. 2005 ([doi:10.2503/jjshs.74.196](https://doi.org/10.2503/jjshs.74.196); title-matched, body not opened as of 2026-11-15 CST) [待确认]. The same Azuma paper's own result for 'Benitaka' is different: a novel *VvmybA1*(BEN) allele attributed to recombination between *VvmybA1a* and *VvmybA3*, not to a TE insertion or excision. How often TEs cause somatic change in vineyards more generally, and how that should be weighed against other mutation classes, is [待确认].

**Other crops.** The maize B73 genome (Schnable et al. 2009) serves as a scale example: TEs make up nearly 85% of that genome and have captured gene fragments (this sentence was not re-opened from the Schnable 2009 paper on 2026-11-15) [待确认]. Further crop-trait examples are not listed here unless a title-matched paper was read for this catalogue.
