# TRIM and LARD

TRIM (terminal-repeat retrotransposons in miniature) and LARD (large retrotransposon derivatives) are non-autonomous LTR-related elements, not separate Wicker orders [待确认].

Witte et al. (2001, abstract, [doi:10.1073/pnas.241341898](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.241341898); Crossref authors Witte, Le, Bureau, Kumar) named and characterised TRIMs and noted typical flanking 5-bp direct repeats at LTR insertion sites. Kalendar, Vicient et al. (2004, abstract, [doi:10.1534/genetics.166.3.1437](https://doi.org/10.1534/genetics.166.3.1437)) named and characterised LARDs from barley elements “formerly called Sukkula elements.” The catalogue wording is therefore “Named and characterised,” not a first-discovery claim for a previously unseen class.

TR-GAG (2015, abstract, [doi:10.1093/gbe/evv001](https://doi.org/10.1093/gbe/evv001); official title *Terminal-repeat retrotransposons with GAG domain in plant genomes: a new testimony on the complex world of transposable elements*) is a related non-autonomous group carrying “only one open reading frame coding for the GAG precursor protein.” (DOI 10.1093/gbe/evv043 was checked and is a human insertion-polymorphism paper, not this one; it is not used.)
