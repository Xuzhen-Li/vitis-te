# Plant pararetroviruses and Florendovirus

These elements are **not** an LTR order. Wells and Feschotte (2020, full text, [doi:10.1146/annurev-genet-040620-022145](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-genet-040620-022145)) define TEs in a way that “includes endogenous retroviruses (ERVs), but excludes endogenous elements that originate from viruses that do not typically integrate and further propagate in the host germline.” Geering, Maumus et al. (2014, full text, [doi:10.1038/ncomms6269](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms6269); official title *Endogenous florendoviruses are major components of plant genomes and hallmarks of virus evolution*) place Florendovirus as “a new genus of Caulimoviridae.” They therefore sit **outside** the Wicker LTR box in F1, and they must not be drawn under “Order LTR.”

The previous wording that plant pararetroviruses “also have repeat-like ends” is deleted: it was not found in the opened Geering full text or abstract.

## What Geering et al. 2014 actually report

- “A new genus of Caulimoviridae called ‘Florendovirus’, members of which have colonized the genomes of a large diversity of flowering plants, sometimes at very high copy numbers (>0.5% total genome content).”
- “In *Vitis vinifera*, 9% of the endogenous florendovirus loci are located within introns and therefore may influence host gene expression.”
- Table 1 (same paper) lists *Vitis vinifera* genome occupancy 3,152,021 bp, genome fraction 0.65%.
- “Some appear to have had a bipartite genome organization, a unique characteristic among viral retroelements.”
- The paper also notes frequent colocation with TA simple sequence repeats.

A grapevine-centred project already exists in vitis-open-directions (`projects/03-endogenous-florendoviruses/`). This chapter does not copy any private results from that work.

**Teaching rule.** Keep endogenous pararetrovirus / Florendovirus models on their own track. Do not silently merge them into Copia or Gypsy. If they enter a TE working library, record that fact in the curation notes. Using protein classifiers (TEsorter / REXdb or virus-specific HMMs) rather than an LTR-only call is this repository’s teaching rule **[待确认]** as a comparative claim: it is not a sentence taken from the opened Geering text.

An unsourced sentence about a “circular double-stranded DNA virion genome” is not taught here: it was not taken from the opened Geering text.
