# LINE and SINE

LINE (non-LTR retrotransposons) is a Class I **order**. Wicker et al. 2007 ([doi:10.1038/nrg2165](https://doi.org/10.1038/nrg2165), Fig. 1; Fig. 1 values **[待确认]**) list five **superfamilies** under that order: **R2, RTE, Jockey, L1 and I**. They are not orders of their own.

Wells and Feschotte (2020, full text, [doi:10.1146/annurev-genet-040620-022145](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-genet-040620-022145)) describe the usual LINE machinery: ORF2 with endonuclease and reverse transcriptase, target-primed reverse transcription (TPRT), and frequent 5′ truncation of new copies. SINEs are non-autonomous, typically derived from tRNA, 7SL or 5S RNA (Wells and Feschotte 2020, full text, PMC8293684: “Most SINEs are derived from Pol III-transcribed noncoding RNA such as tRNA, 7SL or 5S RNA”; Wicker Fig. 1 lists those three as SINE superfamilies, [待确认]). Wells also gives Alu as a 7SL-derived primate SINE.

This chapter does not teach a grape-specific LINE/SINE abundance ranking or a grape discovery history: no opened source for those claims was found in the sources opened for these chapters .
